Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square iOS app icon artwork for a minimalist, modern writing app.
Primary request: Create a distinctive, creatively designed pen emblem, simple enough to recognize at small app-icon size but with the crafted originality of a premium industrial-design object. Clever, sculptural, memorable silhouette rather than a stock pen pictogram.
Scene/backdrop: pure uniform AMOLED black, full bleed square, no border or rounded-square container.
Composition: one compact central emblem occupying about 64% of the square, generous clear padding, strong balanced diagonal silhouette.
Materials and light: pale silver satin metal, one controlled bright folded edge, subtle restrained dimensional shading. Keep large clean planar faces; not a detailed realistic pen. The creativity comes from the folded geometry.
Color palette: black and platinum, with a tiny deep-crimson accent only at the writing tip.
Constraints: no text, letters, initials, watermark, badge, background gradient, halo, glow, glass bubble, separate decorations, generic clip-art nib, stock toolbar pencil, ornate engraving or visible pen hardware. No photographed physical scene. Produce one finished icon, not a board of options. Modern, quiet, distinctive.
